## 3. Needs the GM's answer

1. **Who runs the Wataniah — Nora or Chang?** The faction note says Nora, a Chosen officer, is its head; the player-facing Taman Hidup letter has Chang calling himself "head investigator of the Wataniah." Options: (a) Nora heads the Wataniah, Chang heads *investigations* under her — the log's own "Wataniah Investigative Bureau (East Sector)" (L247) gives him a clean title; (b) Chang is head investigator of one region and is overstating his rank, which is in character; (c) drop Nora. Changes `Wataniah.md` and one line of `Taman Hidup Wataniah Report.md`.

2. **Chang's letter: keep the published version, or apply the tone-down you asked for?** You asked (L216) for "an overly stressed administrator … tone it down, keep some swears," and the chat produced that rewrite (L224–249). The vault instead kept the original rant with Scribe Eli's strikethroughs. Options: (a) keep as published — the censorship gag is funnier and more Fallout; (b) swap in the toned-down rewrite; (c) keep the published text but fold in the rewrite's two best lines ("a terminal that crashes every time I exhale too hard", "a half-finished complaint from some prick who thinks his missing Brahmin is a crime against humanity").

3. **Is the prison cafeteria matriarch Sejora, Senja, or a third woman?** The log gives the role to Mother Sejora; the vault has Sejora running the Orphanage *elsewhere on the Peninsula* and Senja — "thin, wiry Indian woman", Kancil Orphanage, Bandawang — on the spot. Options: (a) it was Senja all along, and the log's name is drift; (b) Sejora was in Bandawang at the time of the prison and has since moved on, which would date her departure; (c) a third Kancil runs the kitchen. Affects `People/Mother Senja.md` and any prison note.

4. **Which Warden ROBO is canon?** Vault: memory shot, hacked to answer uselessly, a joke gift from the Choos, a mascot, with a data-recovery hook. Log: a functioning administrative machine with a morality arc that determines how the prison falls and who survives into the later campaign. Options: (a) vault only — the log's arc dies; (b) both, sequenced: ROBO *was* the administrator, and what the players see now is what the explosion and a hack left of him, which makes "his memory is shot" the consequence of the prequel and the data-recovery hook the payoff; (c) log only. (b) costs nothing and loses nothing.

5. **Is the Chin Peng analogue a ghoul?** You explicitly said "let's not call him a ghoul or human, let's not pick sides" (L48) — but the timeline decides for you. If he fought in the Great Ghoul Massacre (2130–2132), he is 115+ years out from the campaign; only a ghoul survives that. Options: (a) ghoul — the Great Shame is his war, and the tragedy is that he outlived his cause (strongest fit, and it explains why he hides); (b) human, and move his war to the 2231–2241 UCL arrivals — recent, not legendary; (c) "he" is a title three people have held, which the vault already uses as a Ghoul King rumour and would read as a repeat. Also note the Federation did not exist during the massacre, so there is no Federation to have betrayed him in 2132 — it would have to be the Protectorate, or the UCL abandoning him later.

6. **What is at the far end of the Taman Hidup tunnel?** The brief says an "underground reading room"; the log says "a forgotten pre-war facility … tied to abandoned PCI experiments." The second is impossible as phrased — the PCI is a post-war Federation programme. Options: (a) a pre-war facility that the PCI *later reused*; (b) a pre-war facility with nothing to do with the PCI, and the reading room is a room inside it; (c) just the reading room, and the pre-war angle is dropped.

7. **Kai: CEO of Juta Success, or its on-site manager?** The published report says CEO; your later note in the chat says on-site manager, which is what the cover-up role needs. One word in `Taman Hidup Wataniah Report.md`.

8. **Does the Federation itself run a covert ghoul experimentation programme?** The log's disappearances resolve into a "Federation and PCI Relocation and Experimentation program." The vault already assigns ghoul experimentation to the Protectorate (the Kosongs) and, as a GM secret, to Bandar Buaya's mayor. Options: (a) Federation does it too — three separate programmes, each diluted; (b) the disappeared ghouls are being *sold or transferred* to one of the existing two, which keeps the Federation's sin bureaucratic complicity rather than mad science, and is the darker read; (c) the disappearances are something else entirely.

9. **Do you still want the Korean jungle village?** You asked for it at L273 and the chat never answered. If yes, the open design question is which drift you want: a village that preserves the *forms* it half-remembers (ancestral rites performed by people who no longer know the words, a jesa table set with jungle produce, kimchi fermented from local greens in buried jars — which works in a tropical climate only if buried deep or kept in a cooled cellar, a real constraint worth playing with), versus one that has fused outright with its Malayan neighbours and is now arguing about which half is real. Needs one decision before anything can be written.
