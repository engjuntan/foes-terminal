## Story ideas, parked

### The cache list
The party needs to acquire a cache list at some point. Who holds it and
how they get it is undecided — Faiz and the Ghoul King were both floated
and neither is settled. Not a decision to force; just do not let the
thread drop.

### The Frumentarius
Belongs to a story that takes place **after** this campaign, not inside
it. The shipwreck that carries them can sit in the **2270s–80s**. The
only thing owed now is a timeline entry so the date is fixed before
anything else is built on it.

### The Orphanage of Mothers
A future idea, and **not** connected to the Kancil Orphanage. Keeping the
two apart matters — the Kancil is the GM's own child-labour thread and
already has its framing.

### Round City
Not visited in this campaign. The Caliphate capital, its ring structure
and Bandar Bulat stay as background the party hears about rather than
somewhere they go. Its art exists; its detail can wait.
