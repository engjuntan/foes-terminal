# The Peninsula map — territories in text

A text reading of the GM's hand-annotated faction map ("Malaya — Truly A
WASTELAND"), shared 6 Oct 2026. It is here so agents can check geography
without opening the image. Each territory is mapped to **modern-day
Malaysia** as the GM asked; the place names are reading aids, and the
borders are approximate (read off a hand-coloured map).

**Status: GM-side reference.** The map carries GM secrets (the cache sites,
the "Kingdom" overwrite). It is not a player handout as it stands — see
*Conflicts* below.

---

## Territories

## Other marks

- **"Potential Locations for Lost USMC Supplies"** — four red X's, all inside
  the Great Jungle: (1) Hulu Perak near Baling–Gerik; (2) inland Perak near
  Lenggong / Kuala Kangsar; (3) the Cameron Highlands / Tapah hills on the
  Perak–Pahang border; (4) interior Pahang around Kuala Lipis / Taman Negara.
  **GM secret** (the heist's real prize).
- **Crossed-out note at Tioman Island:** "Future Home Me & Sarah", scribbled
  over in red, "sorry". A personal in-world annotation.
- **Small blue marks off Terengganu**, around the Perhentian and Redang islands.
  Unlabelled; could be islands or ink.
- **A dark route line** running from Kedah down the jungle's western edge
  past KL and south-east through Negeri Sembilan into Johor — most likely
  the pre-war north–south highway or railway.
- **Title:** "Malaya — *Truly A* WASTELAND", a defaced parody of the
  pre-war tourism slogan.
- **Torn letter, bottom left (Malay, fragments).** Visible text, with
  [bracketed] guesses at cut-off words:
  > Kepada … / mesti melindu[ngi] … / nenek moyang yang … /
  > Keturunan kita dan h… / harapan Semenanjung. Ke… /
  > ada saudara kita, atau pengk[hianat] … /
  > Hanya satu kesimpulan boleh dibena[rkan] … / Raja Baru ka[mi] [me]nanti.

  Roughly: "To … must protect … the ancestors who … our descendants and …
  the hope of the Peninsula … are our brothers, or traitors … only one
  conclusion can be allowed … the New King [awaits]." "Keturunan" and
  "Harapan" are also the names of existing vault entries. Scrawled over it
  in red: **"BEWARE THE GHOUL KINGS LIES!!"**

## Conflicts with canon (for the GM)

1. **"KINGDOM" over the Federation.** SCOPE_DECISIONS (~l.1081) and five
   vault notes say the Kingdom of Malaya does not exist in 2247, is never
   named in player-facing text, and appears only in GM callouts. The map
   names it outright, so it can only be shown to players as a future-state
   or in-world propaganda prop — or the overwrite comes off.
2. **"USMC" supplies.** Other sources say pre-war **US Army** caches
   (org-map D13). USMC (Marines) is either a refinement or a slip.
3. **The Ghoul King.** The map ties the Ghoul King to a "New King" and a
   "Throne of LIES" near the Federation capital. Per CLAUDE.md his identity
   is never revealed; this file records what is drawn, and nobody should
   read more into it in player-facing text.
4. **The Caliphate's "west coast" character.** The GM describes Caliphate
   towns as small-town west-coast Malaysian life (6 Oct), but the map puts
   its territory on the east coast. Open question to the GM.
